# Community Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.
Constructive disagreement is welcome; personal attacks, intimidation,
discrimination, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not.

Examples of positive participation include:

- giving specific, actionable, and empathetic feedback;
- respecting different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints;
- taking responsibility for mistakes and repairing harm; and
- keeping technical debate focused on ideas and evidence.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, project
spaces, and public events where someone represents opengorky.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when conduct threatens the community.
Enforcement decisions should be proportional, documented privately, and made
without retaliation.

Report conduct incidents privately to `brendan.ardagh@gmail.com` with the
subject `opengorky conduct`. Do not disclose sensitive personal details in a
public issue.
